The Process

Where We Are

1

Application Submitted

Done. Your home is in the queue.

2

Site Survey

A Sunrun engineer visits your home to determine if your roof and electrical setup meet program requirements. Typically within 1–2 weeks.

3

Survey Results

I'll personally walk you through the results and what your custom system would look like. 4–7 days after survey.

4

System Design & Permitting

If approved, Sunrun engineers finalize your system layout and submit for city approval. This is the longest step — typically 4–6 weeks.

5

Installation Day

The crew arrives, installs everything, and cleans up. Most installs are completed in a single day.

6

Final Inspection & PTO

The city inspects the system and grants Permission to Operate. Once approved, your system goes live and your Edison bill drops to the minimum connection fee.
