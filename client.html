Under the agreement, Sunrun owns the equipment and insures it. That includes roof penetration coverage. If a leak happens because of the installation, Sunrun fixes it — not you. Not your homeowner's insurance. Sunrun.

This is one of the biggest differences between a PPA and buying a system yourself. If you purchased panels outright, the workmanship warranty on the roof typically expires after 10 years. Under Sunrun's program, roof protection is covered for the full 25 years because they own what's on your roof and they're responsible for it.

Before any work starts, Sunrun's engineering team inspects your roof to make sure it can handle the system. If your roof needs work first, that gets addressed before panels go up — not after.
